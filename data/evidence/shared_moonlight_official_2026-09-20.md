# Shared Moonlight — Official Current Evidence

Captured 2026-09-20 from current public Foundation: Galactic Frontier announcements.

## Current schedule
- Servers 1001–1016: September 15–21, 2026.
- Servers 1017–1247: September 21–27, 2026.
- Event timing is server-scoped and may change; Season 2 schedules vary by server and the in-game calendar is authoritative for a specific server.

## Activities explicitly announced
1. Shared Moonlight Sign-In — log in during the event to claim rewards.
2. Bingo Bash — complete tasks on the Bingo board, complete rows, and work toward the grand prize.
3. Moonlight Battlepass — complete daily and target quests to earn Moonlight Points and unlock milestone rewards.
4. Lunar Soil Prospecting — explore lunar ruins, excavate hidden treasures, select a Grand Prize, and advance through 100 layers of lunar soil.
5. Moonlit Treasures — earn Crescent Coins and Full Moon Coins and exchange them in the Moonlit Market.
6. Full Moon Charge-Up — earn Purchase Points and enter daily draws.

## Announced limited rewards
- Exclusive ship skin
- Name frame
- Avatar Frame
- Killing Effect
- Festival crew choice: Holly Nico, Murphy Riley, or Boka Lape

## Important calendar limitation
The developer announced that with Season 2, event schedules vary more between servers and players should use the in-game calendar for their specific server. Do not infer a universal Monday–Sunday event rotation from generic calendar-cycle claims.
