# FGF Intelligence v6.3 — Permanent Operating Rules

## Three intelligence branches
1. Core Knowledge: canonical facts, evidence graph, lifecycle, authority, version/season awareness.
2. Live Observation and Experience: direct gameplay observation, state transitions, actions, outcomes, repeated patterns.
3. YouTube and Community: fallback/enrichment interpretation and strategy evidence.

Human feedback is a learning signal, not a truth source.

## Decision order
Core -> validated Experience -> YouTube fallback.

Strategy/interpretation may include validated Experience alongside Core when it adds useful context.

## Core always wins on established facts
If Core establishes a current fact, observation, learned experience, and YouTube cannot silently override it.
A conflicting direct observation becomes a conflict candidate and triggers revalidation.

## Observation is candidate evidence by default
Promotion requires structural validity, context compatibility, repetition where appropriate, conflict checking, and review/promotion.

## Learning
One observation creates a candidate.
Repeated comparable observations strengthen it.
Five comparable observations are the default validation threshold.
Mixed outcomes prevent automatic validation.
Validated experience never rewrites canonical claims automatically.

## Context
Learned patterns are bound to game version, season, server, energy type, mode, event, and relevant fleet/champion context when available.

## YouTube
Use YouTube only after Core does not establish the requested information.
Preserve original video URL and label creator/community interpretation.
Never auto-promote transcripts to canonical truth.
Preserve conflicts.

## Feedback
UI: Correct / Needs improvement.
Feedback triggers investigation; it does not prove a fact and does not delete knowledge automatically.

## Resource and privacy rules
Local observation is capped at two hours per day.
Raw video is temporary by default.
Prefer structured events and small evidence captures.
Do not upload raw gameplay unless explicitly enabled.
Unknown screens fail closed.

## Live-first rule
Live observation and experience are production priority. YouTube ingestion can be populated gradually.

## Version
6.3.0 is the Live Experiential Intelligence architecture and policy layer.
Future changes must preserve these rules unless deliberately versioned and benchmarked.
